Egypt National Qualifier

Where students build
real IoT solutions.

The IoT Innovation Challenge is a 12-week national competition where student teams collaboratively develop Internet of Things solutions addressing real-world problems. Participants create innovative, functional IoT prototypes integrating hardware, embedded systems, wireless communication, and cloud platforms.

This national qualifier serves as Egypt's gateway to the Afro-Asian Forum for Innovation and Technology, where top teams represent the country on an international stage — gaining access to industry professionals, internship opportunities, and global recognition.

Organised by TechVerse · Nextera Education, and part of the Smart Egypt National Youth Innovation Program.

Three Phases

Twelve weeks.
Real results.

A structured, mentor-supported journey from ideation to live national finals — with IoT development kits provided.

1
Phase 1 · Weeks 1–3
Team Selection & Solution Design
Teams are onboarded and complete an Ideation Bootcamp, locking in their IoT solution concept across one of the three national challenge domains.
  • Ideation Bootcamp to frame your challenge
  • Select domain: Agriculture, Energy, or Smart Systems
  • Define your IoT solution concept
2
Phase 2 · Weeks 4–10
Prototype Fabrication & Development
Teams receive IoT development kits and build working prototypes with cloud integration and dedicated mentor check-ins throughout.
  • IoT dev kits provided to every team
  • Mentor check-ins on a scheduled basis
  • Cloud integration validated end-to-end
3
Phase 3 · Weeks 11–12
Judging & National Championship
Final prototypes are independently judged. Top finalists present live at the National Finals. Awards announced in a formal ceremony.
  • Independent panel of expert judges
  • Live presentation at National Finals event
  • Formal awards ceremony
